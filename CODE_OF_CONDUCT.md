# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge
We are committed to making participation in APIVault a respectful, welcoming, and harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of experience, identity, background, or contribution level.

## Expected behaviour
- Be respectful, patient, and constructive.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Welcome questions and differing perspectives.
- Give actionable feedback and assume good intent.
- Respect privacy; never publish another person's private information.

## Unacceptable behaviour
Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, deliberate intimidation, doxxing, and disruptive or abusive conduct are not acceptable.

## Enforcement
Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing this code. They may remove, edit, or reject contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation for behaviour they consider inappropriate.

To report a conduct concern, contact the repository owner through the contact options on the GitHub profile. Do not report security vulnerabilities publicly.

This code of conduct is adapted from the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1.
